# Smart Billing Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Smart Billing processes account details, store details, product inventory, customer names/phone numbers, invoices, payment-method labels, and product images to provide billing and reporting features. It does not process card numbers, UPI credentials, or bank credentials.

Company-mode data is transmitted over HTTPS to the configured API and stored in the company's MongoDB/Cloudinary infrastructure. Authentication tokens and cached business data are stored on the device. Authorized company users can access data belonging to their account.

Data is retained according to the deploying company's legal and operational policy. Account access, correction, export, and deletion requests must be handled by the deploying company's administrator. Service providers may include the selected hosting provider, MongoDB, Cloudinary, email provider, and Expo/EAS.

The app requests camera permission for barcode scanning and photo-library permission for product images. These permissions are used only when the user invokes those features.

This draft must be reviewed for the company's jurisdiction, retention rules, legal entity name, address, support contact, subprocessors, and deletion procedure before it is hosted or submitted to an app store.
